What to Expect at the Meet & Greet

The meet and greet is a relaxed, informal visit where Oli comes to your home to meet you and your dog. It usually takes around 20 to 30 minutes. We'll get to know your dog's personality, discuss their routine, any dietary needs or medical conditions, and work out the best schedule for walks or daycare. It's also a chance for your dog to get comfortable with us before their first session. There's absolutely no pressure — it's just about making sure we're the right fit for you and your pup.
