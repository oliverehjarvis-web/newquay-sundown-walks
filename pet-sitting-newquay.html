Go Out for Dinner. I'll Look After Your Dog.

You're on holiday in Newquay. You've spotted a great restaurant for tonight — but your dog can't come, and you can't leave them alone at the holiday let. I get this all the time.

I come to wherever you're staying — holiday let, hotel, Airbnb, caravan, doesn't matter — and hang out with your dog for the evening. Cuddles, a bit of play, a short walk if they're up for it. I send you photos so you're not sat in the restaurant worrying. You come back when you're ready. No rush.

One of our customers booked us while they went to Rick Stein's. Said it was well worth it. That's the kind of thing this is for.

How It Works

Drop me a WhatsApp or give me a ring — even same-day is usually fine. I'll come to your accommodation, meet your dog, and settle in. You head out. I stay until you get back. That's it. £20 an hour, no hidden fees, no minimum booking.

Pet Sitting FAQs

Can you come to my hotel or holiday let?

Yes! We come to any accommodation in the Newquay area — holiday lets, hotels, Airbnbs, caravan parks, campsites. Wherever you're staying, we'll be there.

How far in advance do I need to book?

We recommend booking before your trip so we can guarantee availability, but same-day bookings are often available. Just drop us a WhatsApp message and we'll do our best to help.

What if we're late back from dinner?

No problem at all. We charge by the hour and are completely flexible. Enjoy your dessert, have that extra drink — your dog is in safe hands and we'll stay as long as you need.
