Dog-Friendly Restaurants in Newquay: The Complete Guide

Updated 23 March 2026

Newquay is one of the most dog-friendly towns in Cornwall, and that extends to its restaurants, pubs and cafes. Whether you are a local looking for your next regular haunt or a visitor wanting to dine out with your dog in tow, this guide covers the best spots in town where your four-legged companion is genuinely welcome.

We have put together this list based on our own experience as dog walkers and pet sitters working across Newquay every day. All venues have been visited and confirmed as dog-friendly, though policies can change — it is always worth a quick phone call ahead if you are visiting at peak times.

The Best Dog-Friendly Restaurants and Cafes in Newquay

The Lewinnick Lodge

Perched on the clifftop between Fistral and Crantock, The Lewinnick Lodge is one of Newquay's most impressive dining spots. Dogs are welcome in the bar area, which shares the same stunning panoramic views of the Atlantic as the main restaurant. The menu covers everything from hearty Sunday roasts to fresh Cornish seafood, and the cocktail list is worth exploring too. If the weather is kind, the terrace is a beautiful place to sit with your dog while watching the sunset over Fistral. It is a popular spot, so booking ahead is recommended, especially during summer evenings and weekends.

The Fort Inn

Tucked away on Fore Street near the harbour, The Fort Inn is a proper Cornish pub that has been welcoming dogs for years. The atmosphere is relaxed and friendly, with a good selection of local ales and solid pub grub including burgers, pies and fish and chips. Dogs are welcome throughout the bar areas, and there is usually a water bowl behind the bar. It is a great choice for a casual lunch or an evening meal without any fuss, and the prices are reasonable by Newquay standards.

Cafe Cloud

Cafe Cloud on Fore Street has become a favourite with Newquay locals and their dogs. This independent cafe serves excellent coffee, homemade cakes and a brunch menu that keeps people coming back. Dogs are welcome inside and the staff are known for making a fuss of visiting pooches. It is a smaller venue, so it works best with one well-behaved dog rather than a pack. Ideal for a morning coffee stop or a light lunch before heading to the beach.

The Boat House

Sitting right on the harbourside, The Boat House offers one of the best locations for a meal in Newquay. Dogs are welcome on the outdoor terrace, which overlooks the harbour and out to sea. The menu leans towards seafood, as you would expect from the setting, with fresh catches and classic dishes done well. During the summer months, the terrace fills up quickly, so arriving early or booking a table is a good idea. Even in the cooler months, it is a lovely spot for a sheltered lunch with harbour views.

Flounders

If you are after proper fish and chips by the sea, Flounders near Towan Beach is the go-to choice. While dogs cannot go inside the restaurant area, the outdoor seating is fully dog-friendly and offers views across the beach. The fish is fresh and the portions are generous. It is the kind of place where you can sit with your dog after a beach walk and enjoy a well-earned meal without needing to dress up or book ahead. Perfect for a relaxed, no-fuss lunch.

Bush Pepper

Located on Cliff Road, Bush Pepper has built a loyal following for its brunch and lunch menus. Dogs are welcome inside, and the staff always have treats on hand. The menu changes regularly but expect dishes with a modern twist using locally sourced ingredients. Their eggs Benedict is particularly good, and the coffee is some of the best in town. It is a relaxed, friendly spot that works well for a late morning brunch with your dog before exploring the coastal path nearby.

The Stable

The Stable specialises in pizza and proper West Country pies, paired with an impressive selection of local ciders. Dogs are welcome inside, making it a solid option for an evening meal. The pizzas are cooked in a proper oven and the pies use quality local ingredients. It is a laid-back, informal setting where dogs fit right in. Particularly good on a rainy evening when you want something hearty without the formality of a sit-down restaurant.

When Your Restaurant Does Not Allow Dogs

As dog-friendly as Newquay is, some of the town's finest restaurants do not permit dogs indoors. Places like Rick Stein's Fistral and The Fish House Fistral offer exceptional dining experiences, but they are not an option if you need to bring your dog along.

Tips for Dining Out With Your Dog in Newquay

  • Book ahead and mention your dog. Most dog-friendly venues are happy to accommodate, but letting them know in advance means they can seat you in the right area and avoid any surprises.
  • Arrive early. Dog-friendly tables, especially outdoor terraces in summer, fill up fast. Arriving before the rush gives you the best chance of a good spot.
  • Bring a water bowl. Many pubs and restaurants provide water bowls, but carrying a collapsible one means your dog is sorted wherever you go.
  • Keep your dog under the table. Even in the most welcoming venues, other diners appreciate dogs that stay calm and tucked away. A blanket or mat your dog is familiar with helps them settle.
  • Avoid peak times if your dog is nervous. A busy Friday evening in a packed pub is not ideal for an anxious dog. Quieter lunchtimes or early evening slots tend to work better.
  • Bring treats and a chew toy. A long-lasting chew can keep your dog happily occupied while you enjoy your meal at a leisurely pace.
