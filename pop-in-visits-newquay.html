Dog Pop-In Visits in Newquay

Sometimes your dog doesn't need a full walk — just a quick check-in, a garden let-out, and a bit of company. Our 15-minute pop-in visits are designed for exactly that. We'll pop round to your home, let your dog out into the garden, top up their water, give them any scheduled food, and make sure they're happy and settled.

What's Included

  • Garden let-out and fresh air
  • Fresh water and feeding if required
  • Cuddles, fuss, and companionship
  • Photo update sent to you
  • Quick health and wellbeing check
  • Secure key holding (fully insured)

Who Is This For?

Pop-in visits are ideal if you're at work for a long day and need someone to break up your dog's afternoon. They're also perfect for lunch-break cover when you can't get home in time, or for elderly dogs and puppies who need more frequent toilet breaks but aren't up for a full walk. If your dog is recovering from surgery or has mobility issues, a gentle garden visit can make all the difference to their day.
