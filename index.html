FAQ

Where do you go for Daycare?

We vary our locations to keep it exciting! We visit Bodmin Moor, expansive woodlands, and dog-friendly beaches across Cornwall depending on the weather.

Do you take unneutered dogs?

We assess each dog individually during our meet & greet. Safety and pack harmony are our top priorities.

What if it rains?

We walk rain or shine! We have professional drying equipment to ensure your dog is returned as clean and dry as possible.
